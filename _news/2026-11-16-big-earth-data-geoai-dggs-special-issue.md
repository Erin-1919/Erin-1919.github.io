**I am serving as a Guest Editor, with Dr. Yue Xu, Dr. Bing Han, and Dr. Alexander Kmoch, for a Special Issue of [Big Earth Data](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/tbed20) titled "GeoAI and DGGS: Advancing Big Earth Data Analytics". Submissions are open until 30 June 2027, and the issue will be published Open Access.**

## About the Special Issue

Earth observation data now arrive faster than they can be integrated. Satellites, airborne platforms, in-situ sensors, and simulations each bring their own resolutions, coordinate reference systems, data structures, and sampling schemes, and that heterogeneity is what limits GeoAI in practice. Discrete Global Grid Systems offer a way through: a hierarchical, cell-based organization of the Earth's surface that gives multi-source data a common spatial frame before any model sees them.

This Special Issue examines what becomes possible at the intersection of DGGS and GeoAI. We are interested in theoretical, methodological, and application-oriented work, and particularly in DGGS as a substrate for AI-ready data organization, multi-source and multi-modal integration, and spatial learning across scales.

Topics include:

- **Data infrastructure**: DGGS-based geospatial infrastructure and interoperability
- **Indexing and organization**: spatial indexing, hierarchical data organization, and AI-ready geospatial data cubes
- **Integration**: multi-source and multi-modal geospatial data integration using DGGS
- **Learning**: GeoAI and machine learning with DGGS, including prediction, pattern discovery, and spatial reasoning
- **Scale**: cloud, distributed, and high-performance computing for DGGS and GeoAI
- **Trust**: reproducible DGGS-GeoAI with uncertainty, provenance, and explainability
- **Digital twins**: Earth digital twins and AI-driven scenario modeling on DGGS
- **Foundation models**: geospatial foundation models, large language models, and AI agents for Earth data
- **Applications**: Earth observation, climate and environmental monitoring, disaster management, urban systems, agriculture, natural resources, and sustainable development
